# Code of Conduct

Atom Bowl is built by and for students. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their age, background or science bowl experience.

## Our standards

Do:
- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers and younger students.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and assume good faith.

Don't:
- Harass, insult, discriminate against or demean anyone.
- Post sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate content.
- Share other people's private information.
- Spam, trash-talk opponents in buzzer rooms, or cheat on shared question content.

## Scope

This applies to the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki) and to Atom Bowl spaces such as buzzer rooms and display names.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules. To report a problem, open an issue (or contact [@shankar-sachin](https://github.com/shankar-sachin) if it is sensitive).

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
