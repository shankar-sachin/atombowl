# Atom Bowl - Info Docs Tutorial

## Step 1 - Go to the Website
- Head to the [Atom Bowl](https://atom-bowl.github.io/Atom_Bowl) website to get started
- Ensure you have JavaScript enabled
- Recommendation: use a mainstream browser like Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari

## Step 2 - Practice
- On the home page, click on the Practice tile, or click [this link](https://atom-bowl.github.io/Atom_Bowl/practice_home.html)
- Put in your desired filters and supertopics for practicing
- Uncheck the `No timer` switch if you would not like a timer for you to buzz. This is based on personal preferences: if you check it, you'll have 5 seconds after the question is read to buzz on tossups and 20 seconds after the question is read to buzz on bonuses (this is purely to emulate true NSB situations) 
- Click the `Advanced Filters` button to explore 20+ subtopics of the main NSB topics
- When you're ready, choose from the 6 different game modes at the bottom
- The "moderator" will read out the question (Note: text and speech will not be 100% synced) and text will follow in typewriter format, letter by letter, when you know the answer, buzz using the spacebar or click on the question
- Autocorrect is powered by AtomCorrect(R) which uses TypeScript to create a perfect auto-correcting system (turn off in settings)
- This experience is powered by the Atom Engine, which works on TypeScript to create powerful autocorrect features and synced text and speech

## Step 2a - Practice Rules (NSB rules)
- Buzzing correctly on a tossup will result in 4 points added to your score
- Buzzing correctly on a bonus will result in 10 points added to your score due to change in difficulty
- Buzzing incorrectly after the question is read will result in no change to your score
- However, buzzing before the question is read and getting it incorrect will result in an interrupt and a penalty of -4 points from your total score
- You will have 5 seconds to buzz after the question is read on a tossup and 10 seconds after a bonus
- Upon buzzing, you will have 5 seconds to type in your answer (as per NSB rules, it should be 2 seconds, but due to the difficulty of typing so fast, we have buffered it 3 additional seconds)
- Questions will appear in the order: Tossup -> Bonus -> Tossup -> Bonus 
- One selected question in the Practice Home filters results in one Tossup-Bonus pair

## Step 3 - Question Bank
- Explore our vast bank of 25K+ questions, more than any other science bowl prepping website!
- Head there now using the nav on the website or [here](https://atom-bowl.github.io/Atom_Bowl/question_bank.html)
- The question bank consists of 25,658 total questions (TU + BO) across two formats: text and image
- There are currently 11,046 total questions (TU + BO) in Bank A (image format)
- There are currently 14,528 total questions (TU + BO) in Bank B (text format)
- We recommend using text format questions from Bank B as they are closer to true NSB conditions than reading an image as we all have different reading speeds
- Bank B is aggregated from both official nationals DOE questions along with regional questions while Bank A is only DOE nationals questions
- We are working to create a Bank C with niche invitational and smaller regional packages before we deprecate Bank A

## Step 6 - Settings
- Customize your preferences in Settings
- Visit it using the nav on the website or [here](https://atom-bowl.github.io/Atom_Bowl/settings.html)
- Just change what you need and go back to practicing, no saving necessary
- Everything is cached and stored on your browser, so you don't need to update your preferences every time you refresh the page
- Use Ctrl+Shift+R or the `Reset to Defaults` button to reset to default settings and preferences
- Change the browser voice setting to a different voice
- Turn on the extremely powerful AtomCorrect(R) powered by TypeScript

## Step 7 - Game Clock
- Click the `Buzzers` dropdown and then select Game Clock or click [this link](https://atom-bowl.github.io/Atom_Bowl/game_clock.html)
- Change the team names and start a variety of different timers and clocks equipped with notifications and sound beeps
- Add tossup and bonus scores along with penalties

## Step 8 - Buzzer Rooms
- This will be split into two sections - Moderators and Players
### Players
- To join a buzzer room, get the code or the link invite from your moderator and paste it into the join box [here](https://atom-bowl.github.io/Atom_Bowl/buzzer_rooms.html)
- The teams will auto-update and you will be able to select your team
- Enter your display name and click join
- Once the host starts the game, hit the `spacebar` to buzz or click the buzzer box
- You will be able to see the timer controls and who buzzed or outbuzzed you
- Just click the `Leave Room` button or Ctrl+W to leave the room
### Moderators
- To create a room, select the `Create Room` button or head [here](https://atom-bowl.github.io/Atom_Bowl/buzzer_create.html)
- Enter the team names, your name, and whether you want to follow NSB official rules or not
- If you don't want to, you will get a dropdown for advanced settings
- Once in, share the join code with your players by clicking the `Copy` button to get an invite or copy-pasting the join code
- Click the `Open Tossup` button to open a tossup and click the topic you're using
- Click the `Open Bonus` button to open a bonus and select which team you're opening the bonus for
- You can always end the room by clicking the `End Room` button or hitting Ctrl+W
