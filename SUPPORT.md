# Support

- **How do I use Atom Bowl?** Read the [usage guide](info_docs.md) or the [wiki](https://github.com/shankar-sachin/atombowl/wiki).
- **Found a bug or have an idea?** Open an [issue](https://github.com/shankar-sachin/atombowl/issues). Include steps to reproduce, your browser, and a screenshot if you can.
- **Security problem?** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md): open an issue labeled `security` and `urgent`.
- **Want to contribute?** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Account trouble?** Use "Forgot password" on the account page first. If that fails, open an issue without sharing your password.

Atom Bowl is maintained by students in their spare time, so replies may take a few days.
